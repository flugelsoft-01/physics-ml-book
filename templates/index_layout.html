The Standardized 6-Part Engineering Anatomy

Every capstone in this book adheres to a disciplined engineering workflow:

1. The Physical Principle
Governing PDEs/ODEs rendered in KaTeX, boundary & initial values, and dimensionless numbers ($Re$, $Pe$, $CFL$).
2. Industrial Context & ROI
Real-world engineering bottlenecks, operational failure modes, and quantified financial impact.
3. Synthetic Physics Solver
Self-contained, deterministic Python code generating physical ground-truth without broken external URLs.
4. Physics-ML Workflow
Feature engineering, baseline ML vs. PINN/physics-constrained surrogate, and conservation-law residual checks.
5. Diagnostics & Visualization
Interactive Plotly visualizations and high-DPI publication figures inspecting field errors and residuals.
6. Production Deployment Notes
Inference latency benchmarks, edge hardware budgets, and real-time physical safety guardrails.
