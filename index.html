1. The Physical Principle
Governing PDEs/ODEs rendered in KaTeX, boundary & initial values, and dimensionless numbers ($Re$, $Pe$, $CFL$).

Interactive Plotly visualizations and high-DPI publication figures inspecting field errors and residuals.

Inference latency benchmarks, edge hardware budgets, and real-time physical safety guardrails.
